About Youth Development Organization

Who We Are

Youth Development Organization (YDO) is a registered Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) in Tanzania dedicated to empowering communities through sustainable development initiatives. We focus on addressing critical issues including environmental health and widespread unemployment, particularly among youth and women.

Our History

Established under the Non-Governmental Organizations Act, No. 24 of 2002, YDO was founded on the recognition that government resources alone are insufficient to meet societal needs. We bring together passionate individuals committed to fostering a society grounded in social justice, fraternity, and gender equality.

Operating Area

Based in Temeke Ward, Dar es Salaam, our organization operates nationally across Tanzania, reaching both urban and rural communities.

Core Values

  • Patriotism: Dedication to national development
  • Integrity: Honest and transparent operations
  • Accountability: Responsible stewardship of resources
  • Social Justice: Equity and fairness for all
  • Gender Equality: Equal opportunities for all genders

Membership

We welcome voluntary membership from individuals 18 years and above who support our vision and mission. Our membership includes:

  • Founder Members - Visionary leaders who established our organization
  • Ordinary Members - Committed individuals joining our mission
  • Honorary Members - Distinguished contributors to our cause
