Office Bearers

👤

Chairperson

Presides over General Meetings and serves as the organization's spokesperson. Oversees daily activities and serves as bank account signatory.

  • Preside over General Meetings
  • Organizational spokesperson
  • Oversee daily activities
  • Bank account signatory
📝

Executive Secretary

Manages organizational communications, documentation, and administrative operations. Keeper of all organizational records and assets.

  • Call and arrange meetings
  • Manage correspondence
  • Keep organizational documents
  • Maintain asset records
💰

Treasurer

Manages all financial operations including budgeting, accounting, and financial reporting. Ensures transparent financial management.

  • Receive and manage funds
  • Prepare budgets
  • Financial record keeping
  • Prepare financial reports

Board of Directors

The Board of Directors consists of the Chairperson, Executive Secretary, Treasurer, and 6 additional members appointed by the General Meeting. The Board provides strategic direction and governance oversight.

Term of Office

5 years with possibility of re-appointment for another 5-year term

Meetings

Held twice per year or during emergencies as needed

Quorum

2/3 of Board members required to conduct business

Key Functions

Make rules, protect organization image, recruit staff, develop strategic plans, and oversee projects

Governance Structure

General Meeting

Supreme organ composed of all organization members
