Frequently Asked Questions

Who can become a member?

Any individual aged 18 years and above, of sound mind, who is ready to support our vision and mission can become a member. International membership requires at least 3 members with at least 2 Tanzanians as founders.

What are the membership categories?

We have three types: Founder Members (founders), Ordinary Members (regular members), and Honorary Members (distinguished contributors).

How can I support YDO?

You can become a member, attend our events, volunteer, make donations, or help us raise awareness about our programs and initiatives.

Are there age restrictions for different programs?

While general membership starts at 18 years, some programs may have specific age requirements. Contact us for details on specific initiatives.

Where do I find information about current programs?

Visit our Programs page on this website or contact our office directly. We regularly update information about ongoing initiatives.
